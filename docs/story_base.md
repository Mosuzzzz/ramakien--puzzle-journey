# Ramakien: Puzzle Journey

## Narrative Structure
The game follows the core Ramakien conflict: Phra Ram must travel, solve problems, and overcome obstacles after Sida is taken to Lanka by Thotsakan. Each chapter gives the player a clear goal, a short story beat, a puzzle set, and either a mob encounter or a boss-style challenge.

## Chapter 1: The Royal Peace
Phra Ram lives peacefully in the kingdom, and the player learns movement, interaction, and basic puzzle controls. A messenger arrives with troubling news about Lanka and Sida, creating the first mission: gather supplies and prepare for the journey.

### Mission flow
- Talk to the king and key NPCs.
- Collect the first clue item.
- Solve a simple tutorial puzzle.
- Unlock the travel gate.
- Begin the journey.

## Chapter 2: The Forest Path
Phra Ram, Phra Lak, and Hanuman travel through a forest filled with branches, hidden signs, and small yak guards. The player must find the correct route by reading clues, matching symbols, and identifying safe paths .

### Mission flow
- Explore branching forest paths.
- Find map fragments.
- Avoid or defeat small mobs.
- Solve a symbol-matching puzzle.
- Unlock the next area.

## Chapter 3: The First Battle Gate
The heroes approach a guarded passage to Lanka. The gate is protected by Thai-style giant soldiers, and the player must clear the area by solving a short combat-puzzle hybrid challenge .

### Mission flow
- Fight a small wave of mobs.
- Trigger a locked gate event.
- Answer a knowledge puzzle to disable the gate seal.
- Move into the next chapter.

## Chapter 4: The Miryap Zone
This chapter is inspired by the classic ศึกไมยราพ episode, which is a natural place for a battle-heavy mission. 
Hanuman enters a tricky underground or magical zone, and the player navigates traps, hidden switches, and enemy guards to rescue Phra Ram.

### Mission flow
- Enter the underground zone.
- Find hidden switches.
- Beat or bypass mob groups.
- Solve a maze puzzle.
- Rescue the trapped hero.

## Chapter 5: Lanka Infiltration
Hanuman scouts Lanka while the player searches for clues about Sida’s location. This chapter focuses on stealth-like movement, dialogue, and environment reading instead of direct fighting.

### Mission flow
- Sneak past yak sentries.
- Collect clue tablets.
- Decode a room password.
- Open the prison route.
- Reveal Sida’s location.

## Chapter 6: The Prison of Sida
The player reaches the place where Sida is held and must solve a final set of learning-based puzzles to unlock her chamber. The challenge should feel dramatic but still easy enough for grade 2–6 players .

### Mission flow
- Find 3 clue pieces.
- Combine them into a code.
- Open the prison seal.
- Trigger a short escape scene.

## Chapter 7: The Final Escape
After rescuing Sida, the group must escape Lanka while enemy mobs appear in their path. This chapter acts as the final test of everything learned so far: movement, puzzle solving, and light combat.

### Mission flow
- Survive the final enemy waves.
- Solve the last gate password.
- Reach the exit portal.
- Trigger the ending cutscene.

## Ending
The heroes return safely, and the game ends with a message about courage, teamwork, and using knowledge to overcome challenges. Educational escape-room design recommends a debrief-style ending, so the game can briefly summarize what the player learned .

## Godot Mission Logic
For Godot, each chapter can be built as a separate scene with:
- A chapter intro cutscene.
- One main objective.
- A puzzle set.
- A mob/boss encounter.
- A completion trigger.
- A transition to the next scene.

That structure keeps development manageable and makes the game easy to expand later.
