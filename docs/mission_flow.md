# Mission Flow
## Ramakien: Puzzle Journey

The game is structured as a sequence of chapters, and each chapter follows the same basic loop: explore, find clues, solve a puzzle, unlock the next area, and move forward.

## 1. Chapter Start
- Show a short story scene.
- Give the player the chapter goal.
- Introduce the area and any new mechanic.

## 2. Exploration
- Player moves through the map.
- Player talks to NPCs.
- Player checks objects for clues.
- Hidden items or symbols may appear in the environment.

## 3. Puzzle Phase
- Player solves a simple knowledge puzzle.
- Puzzle types can include matching, counting, decoding, or ordering.
- Correct answer gives a key, code, or unlock condition.

## 4. Combat / Obstacle Phase
- If the chapter includes mobs, the player must fight or avoid them.
- Small yak mobs block the path.
- Boss chapters end with a stronger enemy encounter.

## 5. Progression Check
- Unlock the door, gate, or next scene.
- Save chapter progress.
- Move to the next chapter or cutscene.

## Chapter-by-Chapter Flow

### Chapter 1: The Royal Palace
- Learn movement and interaction.
- Receive the rescue mission.
- Collect the first clue.
- Unlock the travel path.

### Chapter 2: The Forest Path
- Search the forest.
- Find map pieces.
- Solve a route puzzle.
- Pass the forest gate.

### Chapter 3: The Battle Gate
- Fight small mobs.
- Disable the gate seal with a puzzle.
- Enter the next area.

### Chapter 4: The Miryap Zone
- Explore a maze-like underground area.
- Use switches and hidden paths.
- Defeat or bypass enemy groups.
- Rescue the captured hero.

### Chapter 5: Lanka Infiltration
- Sneak through Lanka.
- Collect clue tablets.
- Decode the prison route.
- Find Sida’s location.

### Chapter 6: Sida’s Prison
- Combine all collected clues.
- Enter the final password.
- Unlock the prison chamber.

### Chapter 7: Final Escape
- Survive the final mobs.
- Open the exit gate.
- Trigger the ending cutscene.

## Mission Logic for Godot
You can build each chapter as a separate scene with:
- Chapter intro cutscene.
- Quest objective.
- Puzzle trigger.
- Mob encounter or boss fight.
- Completion signal.
- Scene transition.

This makes the project easier to manage and expand later.
